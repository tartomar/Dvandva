Support

Help with Dvandva on iPhone, iPad and Android

Need a hand? Write to phone.tarunt@gmail.com and say which device and version you use. We reply as soon as we can.

No sound on iPhone

Dvandva follows the side switch. If the switch shows orange (silent), turn it back and raise the volume.

How to play

Open the game and tap Fight to start a duel. Draw back and release to shoot, choose your astras from the quiver, and win two rounds out of three. The How to Play screen in the game explains the rest.

Your data

Dvandva works offline, has no accounts and collects nothing. See the Privacy Policy.
