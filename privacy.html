Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026  ·  Last updated: September 2026

The short version: Dvandva does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Dvandva is a fully offline, local game. It does not require an internet connection, does not create accounts, and does not communicate with any server.

Device storage

The game stores a small amount of data locally on your device using Android's standard storage — specifically your in-game preferences such as sound settings. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-party services

Dvandva does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or social SDKs. No data is shared with any third party.

Children

Because we collect no personal data at all, Dvandva is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date revised. As long as the app remains data-free, this policy stands as written.
